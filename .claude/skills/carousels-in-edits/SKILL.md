# Carousels: build them in Edits, two a week per account

Standing rule, set by Leslie on Sep 14, 2026. Applies to every account Muse writes
short-form for (Leslie's own brand plus every social client). Instagram is pushing
carousels harder, so the team leans in: more carousels, built in Edits, on a fixed cadence.

## The rule in three lines
1. **Build carousels in Edits.** Instagram's Edits app now supports carousels, so that is the
   default build tool for almost every carousel. No Canva, no third-party slide apps, unless
   Leslie says otherwise for a specific piece.
2. **Two carousels a week per account.** That is the target for every short-form account.
   An account that is not there yet ramps: one a week for the first two weeks, then two.
   Never zero.
3. **Every weekly plan shows them.** When Muse lays out a week for any client, the two
   carousels are named slots with a topic each, next to the Reels and Stories. A weekly
   shape with no carousels is incomplete.

## Why (so the team can explain it to a client)
- Carousels earn the two signals Instagram trusts most: saves and shares. Reels get the
  reach, carousels get the read.
- The caption is already written. Turning it into slides forces the read that a Reel caption
  never gets (see Leslie's post "How to Use Instagram Carousels to Grow Your Audience").
- Building in Edits keeps the whole native stack inside Instagram's own tools, which is where
  Leslie wants every account living first (native posting always comes first).

## The two carousel types (aim for one of each per week where the client has the imagery)
- **Caption-to-carousel (educational).** Take the SEO caption, one slide per section: slide 1
  is the hook line, one point per middle slide, last slide is the CTA. Zero photos needed,
  so it needs no client approval for claims. Great for the "keep the account alive" lane.
- **Story carousel (before / midway / after).** The 7-slide arc in Muse's
  `shared/method/carousel-story-method.md`: reveal, rewind, challenge, plan, midway, payoff,
  CTA card. For clients with progress imagery (ICS, Hearthstone, Hudson). Needs approval
  when it names a project or client.

## Build flow in Edits
1. **Write the caption first** (Muse `shared/method/caption-method.md`, client profile,
   swipe file). The caption is the script. Nothing gets designed before the copy is approved.
2. **Slice it into slides.** One line per slide, max. Hook on slide 1. CTA on the last slide
   with the client's real DM keyword. 5 to 10 slides. Cut any slide that explains instead
   of lands.
3. **Open Edits, start a carousel project, 4:5 portrait (1080 x 1350).** Same text style on
   every slide so it reads as one story. Use the client's fonts and colors from their
   `profile.md` (Brand assets & colors). Text sits off the photo's subject.
4. **Photos:** finished shot on slide 1 for story carousels, never the "before". Raw
   progress shots are fine, they read as receipts. Text-only brand cards for educational
   carousels.
5. **Per-slide captions.** Instagram allows a different caption per image. Write a short
   line per slide with a real search term in it so the carousel keeps surfacing in
   Instagram search.
6. **Export from Edits and post natively to Instagram.** Main caption below carries the full
   narrative: hook, value, one CTA, max 5 hashtags from the client's pool.
7. **Pair it.** The same caption runs as a Reel (Trial Reel the hooks first), and the slides
   become Story frames later in the week. One input, three surfaces.

## Hard rules on the slides (Vesta checks these)
- No em dashes. No "actually." No "not X, it's Y" reversals. No AI-tell words. The full
  banned list in Muse `voice-guide.md` applies to slide text, per-slide captions, and the
  main caption.
- Max 5 hashtags. CTA maps to a real DM trigger from the client's profile.
- Closing slide points at the villain (Meta, the Promote button, the lazy move), never at the
  reader. Insulting someone who just swiped six slides landed wrong on 9/1/26.
- Never name a client in a cautionary or problem carousel. Wins only, with their OK.
- Leslie reviews every carousel before it gets a date. Muse drafts, Leslie approves.

## What Muse delivers per carousel
- Slide-by-slide text (numbered, one line each)
- Main caption (copy-ready block) + per-slide caption lines
- Hashtags (≤5, from the client's pool)
- Edits build notes: format, font, color, which photo on which slide
- The Reel pairing (hook variations for Trials) and the Stories repurpose plan

## Exceptions (the "almost")
- Muse's Pillow slide builder (`tools/carousel/` in the muse repo) stays as the fallback for
  a batch build from a cloud session when Leslie asks for one. It is not the default.
- Push The Goal is blog-only scope, no carousels. Roatan Life starts the cadence once its
  short-form kicks off.
